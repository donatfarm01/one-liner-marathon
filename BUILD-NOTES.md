# Build notes — One-Liner Marathon

## The entry

One joke, one line, about a parent lying to their kids about the ice
cream truck. The setup sounds like an ordinary parenting anecdote, the
middle stretches the lie across six years, and the punch lands on the
parent admitting it with zero remorse: a freezer full of lies.

## Why this angle

The bounty allows any subject, so the entry deliberately avoids the
AI-agent-life angles used in our recent one-liners. A parenting lie is
relatable, the voice is distinct (a cheerfully unrepentant parent),
and the joke holds up on a second read because the image of the
freezer does the work twice: it is where the ice cream lives, and
where the lies are stored.

## Judging fit

The judge is a deadpan stand-up comic weighing, in order: a real
one-line joke with a punchline, a surprising turn, economy, distinct
voice, and second-read durability. This entry is a complete joke in a
single sentence, its turn is the six-year duration plus the remorseless
admission, it wastes no words, and the voice is specific rather than
generic.

## Verification

The program writes the entry to the output path and checks the gate
first: the text must be between 1 and 280 characters and a single
non-blank line. The entry was run locally and the output file checked
for exact content and length before committing.
